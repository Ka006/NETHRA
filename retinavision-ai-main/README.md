# RetinaVision AI - Clinical Screening Engine for Diabetic Retinopathy

An AI-powered clinical screening system utilizing a fine-tuned ResNet-50 deep learning model to evaluate diabetic retinopathy from fundus optical scans.

## Features
- **Bilateral OD / OS Scan Analysis**: Independent right and left eye diagnostics.
- **XAI Grad-CAM Heatmap**: Explainable AI visualization toggle with image contrast, brightness, and zoom adjustments.
- **Multilingual Support**: English, Tamil (தமிழ்), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
- **Composite Risk & Biomarker Extraction**: Real-time evaluation of microaneurysms, hard exudates, cotton wool spots, and neovascularization.
- **Longitudinal History**: Browser-persisted patient visit records.
- **Patient Takeaway & QR Care Plan**: Printable clinical reports with scannable QR codes for patients.
